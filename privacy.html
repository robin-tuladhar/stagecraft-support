Support Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026.

This policy describes how Stage Craft handles information when you use its presentation features or contact support.

Presentations and local storage

Stage Craft stores project information, imported files, slide previews, and associated media in the app’s local storage. Its embedded office editor processes presentation documents on your device. The app does not require a Stage Craft account to create or present slides.

Your device or backup provider may include app data in backups according to your settings. Files selected from cloud storage and exported copies are also subject to the providers you choose.

Permissions and device features

Depending on the feature you use, the app may request access to photos, the camera, or microphone. Selected files and media are used for presentation features. Motion sensor readings are used for the presenter’s laser-pointer control. You can manage available permissions in iOS Settings.

Internet content and sharing

When a presentation uses an image hosted at an internet address, displaying or exporting it can contact that host. The host receives connection information such as your IP address and the requested resource, under its own privacy practices.

When you export, print, share, or present content on an external display, it is made available to the destination you select. Consider the contents of your slides before sharing them.

Support communications

If you email supportstagecraft@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send. These are processed through our email provider, Google, to respond to your request. Google’s handling of email is described in its Privacy Policy. Send only information needed to explain the issue.

This website

Our support and privacy pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. These pages do not include advertising, analytics scripts, or contact forms.

Retention and deletion

Project data is kept locally while you use the app. Removing a project from the library may leave imported media, previews, or recovery copies in app storage. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data. Separately exported files and device or cloud backups must be managed separately.

Support correspondence is retained as needed to respond to and follow up on your request, resolve disputes, or meet applicable obligations.

To request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence, contact the support address. We cannot remotely access or delete presentations stored only on your device.

Policy updates and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. For privacy questions, contact supportstagecraft@gmail.com.
